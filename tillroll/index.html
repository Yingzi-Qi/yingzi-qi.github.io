Reading errors are corrected first in Excel. After you refresh the corrected workbook, Tillroll reruns lookup checks using item ID, brand, official item name, Style/SKU, quantity, price and currency. Guesses and near-price matches remain marked as lookup uncertainty.

For the final item check, upload an independent list with retailer, item ID, item name, brand, price and currency. Label prices as Unit Price or Line Amount and include quantity where available. Include Order ID or Order Number to match the specific purchase.
