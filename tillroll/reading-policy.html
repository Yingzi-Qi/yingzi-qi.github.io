Reading and lookup policy

Reading errors are corrected before lookup uncertainty is evaluated.

Reading pass

  • Selfridges receipt numbers are checked below the barcode.
  • Harrods references are checked beside the date and time on the right-hand side.
  • Unlabelled item IDs are retained. Repeated brand text is removed from the displayed item name while the original wording remains available for audit.
  • Unreadable, conflicting or guessed OCR is marked Reading error.

Lookup pass

  • Refresh the corrected Excel workbook before rerunning lookups.
  • Item ID is the primary key. Checks compare brand, official item name, Style/SKU, quantity, price, currency and purchase reference.
  • VCA is expanded to Van Cleef & Arpels. Non-official brand descriptions are normalised when supported by the independent reference.
  • Missing brand or item name is looked up from item ID, name, Style/SKU and price. A near-price candidate is marked as a guess.
  • Missing, conflicting or inferred lookup evidence is marked Lookup uncertainty.
  • When a reference identifies a specific branch, the short official address is shown beside the provider name.

The canonical policy is maintained with the Tillroll project source.
