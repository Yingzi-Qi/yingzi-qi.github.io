Motivation

Learning, useful analysis and meaningful contribution.

I want to keep developing through unfamiliar subjects and demanding problems, alongside people I can learn from. Moving from mathematics and physics into global-health modelling gave me experience of learning a new field while contributing to research.

I’m interested in making analysis useful: understanding what a model tells us, recognising its assumptions and explaining the result clearly enough for someone else to use it.

Health is an important thread in my interests, particularly how access, geography and socioeconomic circumstances shape outcomes. Medical technology and evidence-informed healthcare are areas I want to explore further.
