Thin-film analysis

Independent dissertation · University of Warwick

I combined experimental X-ray reflectivity results with published literature to investigate thin-film structure and functional behaviour. The work involved data cleaning, chi-square fitting, MCMC-based modelling and uncertainty assessment.

The outcome was a First-Class dissertation examining layer structure, thickness and roughness. I identified a relationship between thickness and functional behaviour within the analysed study; the dissertation was not published.
