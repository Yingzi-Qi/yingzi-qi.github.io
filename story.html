My Story

From mathematics and physics to global health.

I studied mathematics and physics at Warwick, building a foundation in modelling, numerical methods and working from underlying principles. My independent dissertation combined experimental data, literature and uncertainty assessment to understand thin films.

My interest in public health began during my undergraduate degree. At Oxford, I studied Modelling for Global Health and joined a research team at the Big Data Institute, taking responsibility for an analytical workstream while learning a new field.

There is a visual side to my background, too: I studied art alongside mathematics and physics before university. Independent web projects give me a way to explore how ideas work and how they are presented. My experiences in China and the UK also inform my interest in access and opportunity.
