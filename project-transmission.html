Transmission modelling

MSc research placement · Big Data Institute, Oxford

I used R and Stan to investigate time-dependent transmission patterns using anonymous NHS COVID-19 App data. My contribution included formal analysis, visualisation, a reproducible analytical workflow and original manuscript drafting, with shared software and methodology.

I identified a time-step assumption that changed the model’s built-in generation time. I investigated it through simulation and literature review, then revised the specification with my supervisor’s agreement.

The revised specification is used in a first-author manuscript in preparation. I did not prepare the underlying or processed data.
