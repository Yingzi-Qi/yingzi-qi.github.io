Margin Lab

AI-assisted portfolio project

Margin Lab explores how changing sales and unit costs affects a fictional business. It compares 49 scenarios, showing operating profit, margin and break-even levels.

Implementation and recorded technical checks were performed with an AI coding assistant. The scenarios illustrate relationships between assumptions; they are not forecasts or evidence of commercial results.
